In this project, we visualize and analyze data utilized in our bachelor's project. The onset of the Russian invasion of Ukraine marked a period of significant geopolitical and economic turmoil. With trade embargoes and additional economic sanctions imposed, one would anticipate fluctuations in key economic indicators such as GDP growth and inflation. We utilize data sourced from Eurostat to conduct an analysis. We anticipate observing more pronounced fluctuations in these economic indicators in countries with close trade ties to Russia. Initially, we organize and combine our datasets. Subsequently, we create a variable describing pre-war trade with Russia. This variable is formulated as the aggregate trade with Russia as a percentage of GDP in 2021 for each country. Furthermore, we graph the changes in GDP and inflation followin the war for each country.
